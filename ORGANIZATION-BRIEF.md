## Core Values

### Honesty

ChiPMA favors candid conversations over sanitized promotion. The community is willing to discuss difficult subjects such as bias, sexism, racism, and the real effectiveness of popular product practices. The goal is useful, grounded conversation that helps practitioners move forward.

### Collaboration

Product management depends on collaboration. ChiPMA gives practitioners a peer community and works with organizations that employ product people, building an ecosystem intended to benefit both.

### Community

For ChiPMA, community means knowing fellow Chicago product professionals in person. Members are encouraged to contribute knowledge, time, and support, and to give more than they take.

### Inclusion Across Experience Levels

The values statement says everyone has something valuable to contribute regardless of tenure. This is an important supporting principle and should shape speaker selection, event formats, and site voice.

## Event Philosophy

ChiPMA empowers volunteer organizers to lead with autonomy and a bias toward action. Events can vary in format as long as they uphold the community's values and the following principles:

- **Focus:** Make the subject specific enough to move beyond surface-level discussion and leave attendees with practical takeaways.
- **Distinct topics:** Prefer compelling product problems to trend-following events that only skim a popular subject.
- **Elevate voices:** Rotate facilitators, panelists, and hosts to represent more of Chicago's product community.
- **Opportunity to network:** Reserve time in every event for attendees to meet and talk with one another.
- **Participant value:** A smaller event with a valuable niche topic can be more successful than a broadly marketed event with little depth.
