What brings us together

Better products start with better conversations.

ChiPMA helps Chicago-area product professionals get better at conceiving, designing, building, launching, and optimizing digital products and services.

Our events go beyond polished success stories. We make room for useful debate, specific takeaways, and the kind of peer relationships that make a big city feel a little smaller.

For product managers, product owners, UX designers, product marketers, and everyone working alongside them.
